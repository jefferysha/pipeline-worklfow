# Market Research

Define the audience, market segment, and decision to support. Compare direct competitors,
substitutes, positioning, pricing or adoption signals, and unmet needs using dated sources. Do not
turn a small sample into a market-size claim. Capture facts, hypotheses, and implications separately
so product decisions can be reviewed later.
