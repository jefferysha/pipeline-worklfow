# Deep Research

Define the question and decision first. Prefer primary sources, record source URLs and dates, and
separate verified facts from interpretation. Compare at least two viable alternatives when the
decision is material. Save a concise evidence table, recommendation, unknowns, and follow-up
questions in the active change's research document.
