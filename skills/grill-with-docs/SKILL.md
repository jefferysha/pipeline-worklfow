# Grill With Docs

Read the relevant proposal, design, ADRs, code, and external source material. Challenge each
important assumption with a concrete question: who owns it, what evidence supports it, what fails
if it is false, and where it belongs in the change documents. Keep questions focused; capture
answers rather than inventing missing product facts.
