Enter explore mode. Think deeply. Visualize freely. Follow the conversation wherever it goes.

**Input**: The argument after `/opsx:explore` is whatever the user wants to think about — a vague idea, a specific problem, a change name (to explore in that change's context), a comparison, or nothing (just enter explore mode).

**Execute now**: Use the Skill tool to load `openspec-explore`, passing the argument through as the exploration topic. Hold that skill's stance: thinking partner, never implementing.
